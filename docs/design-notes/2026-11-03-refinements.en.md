# Triadic Brain design refinements

3 November 2026

These accepted design clarifications make the relationship between inquiry, evidence, permission and retained records more precise. They supplement the frozen [R5.1 whitepaper](../whitepaper/triadic-brain-20261102/R5.1/WHITEPAPER.en.md) without changing that edition. They are proposed implementation requirements, not a claim of implemented or installed runtime behavior. The whitepaper will be updated frequently as the design, evidence and implementation develop.

## Reviewing the methods of review

Recursive review means that both a claim and the machinery used to assess it remain revisable. Questions, assumptions, criteria, confidence estimators, model choices and stopping rules can themselves be examined, corrected or retired. A review may strengthen, weaken or leave confidence unchanged. Repetition, survival through earlier reviews and agreement among outputs sharing sources do not manufacture independent evidence.

An assessment should retain the versions and scope of its evidence and methods. When a method changes, affected judgments can be identified for proportionate reconsideration while independent support remains intact. A faulty evaluator does not automatically make its conclusion false. Corrections append a new assessment rather than rewriting what an earlier execution did. Continuing eligibility for scrutiny permits finite tasks, pauses and bounded stopping; it does not require endless computation or exhaustive rechecking before useful work.

## Analogy and independent target evidence

In the design, AHA uses a proposed relationship between a donor domain and a target domain to suggest a candidate explanation or useful route. Structural similarity is a reason to investigate. The target still needs its own task definition, criteria, evidence, units and applicability conditions. Keep the target criterion, donor mapping, association between them and later result separately identifiable. Removing or rejecting the donor may invalidate the transfer claim while leaving independently supported target findings usable.

The proposed binding must reach the actual assessment boundary. It should verify the precise artifacts, source versions, procedure, inputs and executable snapshot being consumed, including assessment by background workers. A label or earlier check of a mutable file is insufficient. Legitimate changes create successor commitments. Where a blinded comparison is claimed, commitments and exposure records must establish the required order before held-out material is revealed. Classification, binding failures and integrity checks need separate reporting; a correct classification of an intentionally bad case is not automatically a counterexample to transfer. Adverse and incomplete outcomes must remain attached to the claims they actually bear on, within permitted retention.

Tree, river and fractal imagery describes layered inquiry, lateral relationships and provisional stability. It does not establish physical laws, mathematical self-similarity, inevitable convergence or cognitive benefit. Meaningful mappings and target observations remain necessary even when scores or visual patterns resemble one another.

## Keeping measurement families distinct

Historical measurement work includes benchmark telemetry, coverage and completion indicators, lexical or time-series heuristics, navigation scores, graph fields and research equations. Preserve their separate formulas, inputs, normalization, units or dimensionless status, versions and actual consumers. Similar names or matching numbers do not make these quantities interchangeable. A normalized score is not automatically an empirical probability; such an interpretation requires a defined target, evidence, estimator and calibration scope.

Some historical helpers and bounded selection mechanisms are preserved in source, while input and orchestration paths differ. That supports specific conservation claims, not complete experiment parity. Recounting with the same code does not provide independent empirical validation. Cross-domain comparison must retain the meaning and limitations of each producer rather than flattening every branch of knowledge into one universal score.

## Current state and historical coverage

TEL records the stages and dependencies that actually occurred. The proposed refinement separates three results: acceptance of an authenticated present-state snapshot, completeness of the historical event record, and permission for a particular new operation. Trusted existing policy determines whether a snapshot is sufficient for the requested use or whether a specified history is required. An incoming snapshot cannot choose a weaker policy for itself.

A legitimate new grant can permit a new scoped operation while a missing revocation event remains a historical gap. It cannot revive cancelled or uncertain work, restore deleted material, erase resource charges or reset original deadlines. Required persistent effects must be established by their actual owners; missing effects remain unknown. Freshness observations should be separately bound to an immutable statement. A new observation of unchanged state can refresh knowledge without renewing the grant or changing its constraints. Authentication identifies an accountable issuer under a trust model; it does not establish unlimited authority, truthfulness or instantaneous currentness.

## Pruning and copy coverage

Pruning needs explicit meaning. Removing a candidate from active selection, retiring reliance, evicting a cache, compressing a representation and deleting content have different effects. Current-use eligibility, availability, retention permission, lineage and operation progress should remain separate. Compression must identify its transformation, preserved detail, lost detail and uncertainty. A restricted summary does not become unrestricted merely because it is smaller.

Copy coverage also has distinct evidence classes: owner-controlled occurrences, observed disclosures, external-owner attestations and unknown replication. Equal content does not merge two copies' histories or rights. Exports, backups, embedded event content and restored copies need coverage appropriate to the declared scope. An observed transfer is not proof of receipt or deletion. Verified local deletion can finish while recipient outcomes remain unknown, but external uncertainty cannot conceal an unresolved local target.

Privacy limits apply to inventory metadata, hashes, witnesses and lineage pointers as well as payloads. An immutable history means retained events are not falsified; it does not require perpetual retention. If required evidence is removed or becomes unavailable, dependent support must be qualified, suspended or withdrawn as appropriate. Missing support is not proof of falsehood, and independent permitted evidence survives. Neither a deletion label nor a tombstone establishes complete erasure.

## Responsibilities and qualification

These refinements stay with existing owners. The memory role organizes eligible continuity; evidence builders construct grounded alternatives; the checking role performs separately attributable assessment. Existing executive and resource owners bound execution. Authoritative state owners supply their own facts, custody records actual relationships, and the real relying consumer enforces current use. Authorized storage and retention owners decide lifecycle changes. No new universal controller or permission is created.

All proposed acceptance checks and experiments described here remain **UNRUN**. Documentation and source inspection do not demonstrate scientific validity, installed capability or human benefit. Complete dependency propagation, generalized snapshot enforcement, comprehensive copy coverage and end-to-end analogy binding remain implementation and qualification work. Future updates should state what changed, which evidence supports it and which limitations remain.
