# Product direction and proposed P2P cooperation

Control Anchor is intended to make AI-assisted work useful under understandable
local rules, with inspectable evidence and attributable human decisions. This
note reconciles the public reviewer component with the maintained product design.
It adds no implementation, study result, funding or delivery commitment.

## Local usefulness and intended reach

A node is an independently controlled local installation. Participation is
optional: each node can refuse work, disconnect or continue locally. Intended
contexts include family groups, community teams, departments, national governments
and global organizations. These are goals, not customers, partners or demonstrated
scale. Household, workplace and community memberships do not confer transitive
permission to share another group's data or tasks.

The existing six-month **USD 100,000 core local integration proposal** remains
unchanged, including its additional-cost caveats. Wider cooperation is staged
work, not an added pilot deliverable or a prerequisite for unrelated installation
work. See the [current pilot](funding/INTEGRATION_PILOT.md).

## Four separate choices

| Choice | Intended meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Task-local custody | Retain the evidence needed to inspect what happened in a supported task. |
| Optional cross-task memory reuse | Select material for later eligible use under its purpose, duration and current permissions. |
| Peer artifact or storage sharing | Disclose or retain selected permitted artifacts across explicitly chosen boundaries. |
| Contributing compute | Let an eligible peer perform a separately authorized job with resource and cost limits. |

None of these choices automatically authorizes training, publishing a conversation
or exporting an entire memory store. Sending and receiving nodes must bind
identity, operation, selected content/purpose, current consent and expiry,
resource limits, retention and onward-use restrictions. Use minimal selected
evidence. Discovery and transport do not inherit policy authority.

Revocation can constrain future permitted use; it cannot promise erasure of every
copy already disclosed. Encrypting a transfer does not hide its plaintext from
the receiving machine entrusted to process it.

## Reusing work without inheriting authority

- **Equivalent completed result:** verify relevant inputs, procedure/control
  versions, freshness, method/model requirements and current rights.
- **Workflow adaptation:** reuse useful structure, bind changed inputs, perform
  affected checks and retain a new attributable result.
- **Cross-domain bridge:** treat a proposed transferable relation as a hypothesis;
  explain its limits and validate it in the destination context, allowing refusal.

Similar prompts do not establish equivalence. A signature identifies an origin
under a trust arrangement, not truth. Copies of one answer are not independent
corroboration and must not satisfy a requested fresh Challenge.

The maintained roles remain Sonya/AEGIS for admission, UCC for effective control
context, CoherenceLattice for evidence/candidate relationships, Sophia for advisory
evaluation, Atlas for explanation, dissent and continuity, and TEL for actual
stage relationships. Each receiving node keeps local custody and decision
authority. A Sophia network is not a central authority over participants.

Usage/cost reporting is separate from billing authority. A reused artifact retains
one producing job and separately recorded receiving uses; do not invent a fresh
generation for each recipient. Showback describes usage and proposed cost
attribution without moving money. Preparation, discovery, review and correction
are part of the cost of reuse.

## Current boundary and a later proof

This reviewer demonstration is synthetic, offline and provider-free. It executes
neither a peer prototype nor live inference. Upstream CI206 separately demonstrated
restricted installed terminal Home and saved/reread provider selection. Protected
provider connection and connected live-model acceptance remain unproved; no
installed capability is added to this reviewer demo.

The passive completed-artifact prototype remains preview-only. Separately,
unmerged PR67's earlier local receiving source passed CI211 (51 cases / 153 phases
at `b1e684b5c81f3df623fd654f1bb4ceeb21788e7b`) with synthetic material and injected
boundaries. Its newer staging successor at
`a4c2f28087038fec13d0417cfa47b6f994f34d87` has 35 authored/unexecuted cases,
including the added metadata-response regression.
Neither result establishes live peer networking, shared compute or independent
corroboration. The [product map](PRODUCT_MAP.md#evidence-edition-and-remaining-joins)
keeps these checkpoints separate; no private prototype or peer code is included.

A first later acceptance intention is two permitted nodes sharing one completed,
non-sensitive artifact while preserving its producing record and a separate
receiving-use history. Include compatible use plus wrong-scope, stale and revoked
rejection; retain an explicitly requested independent Challenge. Disconnects and
uncertain outcomes remain attributable UNKNOWN, without automatic resend.

Fresh remote compute is a separately chosen exchange with its own authority,
resource/cost constraints and receiving review. It does not follow from a passing
artifact-reuse check. These are staged acceptance intentions, not completed
capabilities or universal new launch gates.

## A proposed two-workspace comparison

Use explicitly synthetic workspaces and comparable tasks. Compare fresh work,
a capable ordinary shared library/search/checklist, and provenance-guided reuse.
Give both reuse conditions the same prior library and current evidence. Match
model access, attempt/review budgets, quality criteria and stopping rules.

Include permission, control-version and evidence changes. Authentic bytes can
still carry an obsolete conclusion. The receiver should preserve permitted useful
structure while rejecting unsupported conclusions or actions. Missing evidence
and a demonstrated rule violation require their appropriate, distinct outcomes.

Measure adequate completion, consequential errors, valid abstention, setup,
discovery, all attempts, review/correction burden, assistance, failure handling and
latency. A later remote-compute comparison also needs an eligible local baseline
and transfer, loading, verification, storage and total resource costs. Fewer local
model calls, tokens or charges alone do not prove net savings or lower energy.

Allow the ordinary library or local execution to be equally good or better.
Null and negative results are valid. Two synthetic workspaces can expose
feasibility problems, not establish population benefit or global scalability.
No study execution or additional pilot commitment is authorized by this note.
