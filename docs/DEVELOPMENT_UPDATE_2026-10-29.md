## Volunteer opt-in is separate from permission to execute

Focused synthetic source tests have covered local volunteer opt-in, scoped job
admission, current-authority rejection and reconciliation without resubmission.
They use existing native authorization and custody components. An admitted job
remains held: admission does not reserve resources, release a provider or run a
volunteered model. Different source revisions and test selections keep their own
results; they are not a combined current-product pass.

This is groundwork for optional P2P cooperation, not a running distributed
compute service. Artifact reuse, contributed inference and model training remain
separate choices. Imported work does not become independent corroboration, and
no network-wide efficiency or cost saving is established.
