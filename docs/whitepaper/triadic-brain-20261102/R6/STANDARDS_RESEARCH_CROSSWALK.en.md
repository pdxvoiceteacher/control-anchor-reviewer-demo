## How to read the comparisons

**Formal standard** names a normative specification in its stated scope. **Research term** means vocabulary supported by the cited literature, not scientific consensus about this system. **Implementation pattern** describes an engineering mechanism. **Project proposal** preserves an intended responsibility for which no full conventional equivalent is established. Source records also distinguish authoritative guidance, informational architecture, official implementation documentation and original research. A source can explain a narrow subresponsibility without standardizing the whole family.

**Exact** would require an expressly bounded matching definition; none of the whole-entry comparisons here is exact. **Partial** identifies an overlap and retains the differences. **Distinct** identifies a useful neighboring idea or project-specific responsibility that must not be treated as its synonym. **Unresolved** leaves the correspondence open. No comparison asserts majority use, conformance, priority, originality, outside endorsement or successful implementation. Branded and historical names are not API aliases for the conventional terms.

Most responsibilities and limitations below were already present in the architecture. This companion supplies source connections and compact examples of still-needed consumer records. An example is a proposed way to investigate an existing connection, not a claim that its contract or runtime exists. Integration status stays in the family guide. New external literature does not inherit or improve the project's evidence status.

GUFT remains a growing cross-domain research grammar, not merely a serialization format. AHA remains governed inquiry with donor evidence, disanalogies and target checks, not embedding similarity. TAF remains wider than its current burden proxies. moralWARE and CECP retain normative reasoning, care and recourse beyond access control. The possibility of emergent collective experience remains an open question. Familiar parts neither erase that larger ambition nor validate it.

### Three checks can return three different answers

Related families: [F15](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F15), [F49](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F49), [F50](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F50), [F52](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F52), [F53](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F53).

A garden calculation arrives in a well-formed packet, but its unit describes area while the target expects water volume. Its structure can be valid while its domain interpretation fails. A second packet can have correct structure and meaning while its sharing permission has expired. A proposed validation record therefore reports representation, domain applicability and action authority separately, with the grammar, adapter, source and control versions used. None of these outcomes silently becomes the other. This makes an existing distinction inspectable; it does not select JSON, RDF or SHACL as the Brain's required language.

A missing observation, restricted observation, inapplicable predicate, out-of-domain input and no adequate mapping need different explanations and next steps. A proposed new distinction remains a proposal. The richer historical cases of non-meaning, no asserted relation and lost discrimination remain unresolved where their current consumers are unknown. A generic null field cannot settle them.

### Map a meaning without erasing its source

Related families: [F12](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F12), [F16](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F16), [F17](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F17), [F18](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F18), [F51](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F51), [F55](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F55).

A proposed sense mapping should name both source versions, the claimed relation, supporting passage, scope, reviewer and unresolved alternatives. Start with related or unresolved when interchangeability is not supported. A claim of close interchangeability needs its bounded use stated; an exact equivalence claim needs stronger justification and its downstream implications checked. These are mapping obligations, not a ranking of cultures or people.

For an AHA bridge, keep retrieval resemblance, relational correspondence, adaptation, target prediction, disanalogy and review as separate fields. A donor method that looks similar can still fail the garden's target conditions. Compare the proposed transfer with a target-native or donor-free approach, including human correction burden. The source passages and contrary interpretations remain available where permitted. A compact projection must declare its input/output meaning and omitted alternatives; normalized scores are not automatically calibrated probabilities. No single aggregation rule is selected here.

### Reuse a method without pretending every memory decision is the same

Related families: [F13](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F13), [F14](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F14), [F47](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F47), [F48](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F48), [F68](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F68), [F69](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F69), [F70](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F70), [F80](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F80), [F81](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F81), [F82](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F82), [F84](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F84), [F85](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F85).

A prior garden plan can be retrieved, yet permitted only as background. Its presence in a candidate list is not permission to place it in answer-support context. A proposed review record distinguishes prior exposure, source support for a claim, declared reliance and any later controlled comparison of influence. Word overlap and a model's own account cannot settle individual internal causation.

If a water assumption is withdrawn, record which conclusions depended on it and reconsider those conclusions without rewriting unrelated observations. A surprising method can be nominated separately, with novelty relative to a declared comparison context rather than assumed universal originality. This preserves unexpected discovery without forcing it into the present answer.

For retention planning, declare which retained claims or replay modes need which ancestors. If an essential dependency can no longer lawfully be kept, downgrade or invalidate the dependent replay claim rather than retaining prohibited content by default. A recommendation, actual storage change and later current-use decision remain separate records. This complements foundational consolidation and ethical forgetting; it neither substitutes a garbage collector for PMR nor implies model-weight training.

### Follow authority to the actual consumer

Related families: [F03](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F03), [F04](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F04), [F05](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F05), [F06](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F06), [F21](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F21), [F22](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F22), [F25](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F25), [F29](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F29), [F35](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F35), [F36](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F36), [F38](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F38), [F45](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F45), [F59](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F59), [F62](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F62), [F64](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F64), [F73](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F73), [F75](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F75).

Peer discovery finds an endpoint. Authentication identifies a communicating principal. The receiving owner separately admits an exact task and resource reservation. Returned text then faces the local task's current-use rules. A discovered capability, favorable screen, business assessment or authenticated identity cannot stand in for all four steps. A source-control draft also needs an identified activation owner; generating a plausible interpretation does not activate it.

A proposed consumer record names the action, advisory finding if any, technical decision, accepted control version, authenticated owner, deciding consumer, unsupported check and actual outcome. Business assessment remains advisory: ordinary continuation does not acquire a universal manual-review gate. Preauthorized finite actions remain possible.

For interruption, distinguish one logical operation from its dispatch attempts. Before repeating an uncertain external effect, reconcile the original identifier using the accepting consumer's documented behavior. A current-owner generation must be checked at the protected operation, including after restart, so an obsolete worker cannot commit merely because it still has an old checkpoint. If the chosen consumer cannot reconcile an effect safely, that operation stays unresolved; unrelated permitted work need not stop.

Adapter qualification separately identifies protocol revision, endpoint, model/runtime, native validator, role, disclosure route and retry behavior. For optional confidential execution, identify the attested workload, appraisal, freshness assumptions and key-owner release decision. These records describe questions to resolve, not installed grants, an adopted framework or a universal confidential-computing route.

### Know which evidence was actually observed

Related families: [F28](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F28), [F30](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F30), [F35](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F35), [F37](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F37), [F60](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F60), [F61](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F61), [F63](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F63), [F65](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F65), [F66](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F66), [F67](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F67).

Suppose two dispatch attempts may have produced one effect, while one reply is missing. Count logical operations, attempts, observed effects and unknown outcomes separately. Copying the native event into a host journal does not create a second independent observation. A proposed observation envelope records producer, capture interval, quantity definition, unit, cumulative or interval meaning, gaps, resets and sampling limits. A gap is not zero.

TAC's sampling decision, buffering, retention, export and downshift have separate scopes. Optional CES characterizes supplied events; it does not supply collection permission or a cryptographic signature. TEL records explicit workflow relationships, not hidden chain of thought. Where an export uses a general provenance vocabulary, preserve project-specific evidence-channel types and disclose only the approved graph portion. Omitted or inaccessible ancestry remains incomplete where revealing that fact is permitted; a reference is not a retrieval grant.

A receipt should connect these distinctions to practical inspect, correct, appeal, stop, export and recovery journeys. Proposed future observation includes keyboard and assistive use, errors, assistance and burden. A successful document check is not proof that the real stop control stops an action or that a remedy is accessible or fair.

### Give quantities and efficiency claims an inspectable meaning

Related families: [F39](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F39), [F40](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F40), [F43](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F43), [F46](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F46), [F52](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F52), [F53](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F53), [F78](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F78).

A proposed quantity card names its namespace, source definition/version, quantity kind, unit or dimension, measured-versus-declared status, estimator, uncertainty assumptions, transformation and validity range. Synthetic coordinates stay synthetic. Rosetta views, containment and agency diagnostics remain source-specific research; the card does not certify them as physical quantities.

A TAF coverage ledger should show each canonical term/version, interpretation, normalization reference, weight origin, observed or surrogate status, unmeasured components and excluded burdens. Unknown is not zero. Present component values before a composite and show sensitivity to admissible assumptions. The wider Total Action Functional remains a research program rather than a token, byte or monetary score.

For DeltaSyn, keep exact reconstruction, model-and-data coding objectives and proposed task-semantic fidelity in separate columns with their actual status. Count donor information, residuals, metadata and reconstruction costs. Codec success cannot by itself prove useful semantic consolidation or savings in physical energy.

A proposed comparison manifest names the task population, versions, hardware, warm state, repetitions, quality criteria, failures, measurement boundaries, uncertainty and human burden. Compare appropriate fixed-model, simple-rule and routed approaches, counting the router, review and retries. Judge the result against a declared quality floor and cost-quality tradeoff, not an unexplained rule that every metric must improve. A fixed-workload speedup means lower end-to-end latency while meeting the declared quality floor; quality need not also increase. Scaled-workload throughput is a different comparison. Ownership and access can remain valuable when the cooperative route is slower, and no few-node result forecasts millions of nodes.

### Distinguish a useful review from a reassuring one

Related families: [F07](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F07), [F08](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F08), [F09](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F09), [F10](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F10), [F11](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F11), [F19](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F19), [F20](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F20), [F27](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F27), [F44](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F44), [F56](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F56), [F83](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md#F83).

A future critique comparison should include both a wrong answer repaired by sound counterevidence and a correct answer damaged by misleading criticism. Record correction and corruption separately, with total effort, unresolved findings and exact candidate versions. Distinct role privileges and independent evidence are different properties. A fluent synthesis is not a substitute for either.

Retrosynthesis can offer several explanations for one observation. A useful record keeps the alternatives and identifies a further observation that would separate them. Operational self-monitoring can compare reported state with observed outcomes and ask whether bounded adjustment helps. Subjective or collective experience remains a related open hypothesis, not an alias for successful self-monitoring and not the metric for this check.

Classify a formal counterexample, spurious abstraction trace, empirical failure and review objection separately before choosing a repair. Preserve the witness and the claim it challenges. A stopping recommendation must reach the actual executor or resource owner; premature stopping and continued useless work are both outcomes to inspect. None of these proposed checks is reported as executed here.

Sources were consulted on 2 November 2026. Versioned sources retain their stated edition; live documentation needs an actual revision pinned before implementation. <a id="SRC01"></a>

### SRC01 Measuring Attribution in Natural Language Generation Models

2022-08-02. Type: primary research framework. Source-support evaluation for generated statements; not general factual truth or proof of causal influence. [Primary or official source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.12870v2).

<a id="SRC02"></a>

### SRC02 ACLP: Integrating Abduction and Constraint Solving

2000-03-10. Type: primary system paper. Combines abductive reasoning and constraint solving. Formal candidate explanations are not empirical causal confirmation. [Primary or official source](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0003020v2).

<a id="SRC03"></a>

### SRC03 Case-Based Reasoning: Foundational Issues, Methodological Variations, and System Approaches

1994. Type: primary framework paper, author-hosted. Case-based problem solving separates retrieval, reuse, revision and retention; source does not supply Brain permissions. [Primary or official source](https://www.iiia.csic.es/~enric/papers/Aamodt_1994_Case.pdf).

<a id="SRC04"></a>

### SRC04 Structure-Mapping: A Theoretical Framework for Analogy

1983. Type: primary theoretical paper, author-hosted. Relational structure rather than surface resemblance underlies the proposed account of analogy. [Primary or official source](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/Gentner83.2b.pdf).

<a id="SRC05"></a>

### SRC05 Structure-Mapping: A Computational Model of Analogy and Similarity

Undated source accessed 2026-11-02. Type: official research-project documentation. SME describes correspondences, candidate inferences and structural match scores; scope is the represented comparison. [Primary or official source](https://www.qrg.northwestern.edu/ideas/smeidea.htm).

<a id="SRC06"></a>

### SRC06 SKOS Simple Knowledge Organization System Reference

2009-08-18. Type: formal standard: W3C Recommendation. SKOS distinguishes concept relations. closeMatch needs scoped interchangeability; related or unresolved mappings are safer when that evidence is absent. exactMatch carries stronger consequences, including transitivity. [Primary or official source](https://www.w3.org/TR/2009/REC-skos-reference-20090818/).

<a id="SRC07"></a>

### SRC07 Introduction to Soar

2022-05-08. Type: primary architecture description. Version 9.6 overview of decision-making, memories, impasses, learning and interfaces; not a universal model of human cognition. [Primary or official source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.03854).

<a id="SRC08"></a>

### SRC08 Cognitive Architectures for Language Agents

2024-03-15. Type: primary conceptual-framework paper. Organizes language agents using memory, actions and decision-making; architectural vocabulary, not validation of this integration. [Primary or official source](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.02427v3).

<a id="SRC09"></a>

### SRC09 Reflexion: Language Agents with Verbal Reinforcement Learning

2023-10-10. Type: primary empirical method paper. Task feedback and retained textual reflections guide later attempts without updating model weights; evaluated tasks bound the claims. [Primary or official source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.11366v4).

<a id="SRC10"></a>

### SRC10 Large Language Models Cannot Self-Correct Reasoning Yet

2024-03-14. Type: primary empirical paper. In studied reasoning settings, intrinsic self-correction without external feedback sometimes degrades answers; not a theorem about every model or future system. [Primary or official source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01798v2).

<a id="SRC11"></a>

### SRC11 A Truth Maintenance System

1979-06-01. Type: primary MIT AI memo. Maintains reasons for beliefs and revises assumption-dependent beliefs; truth maintenance names an algorithmic responsibility, not omniscience. [Primary or official source](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/5377b306-4ecc-4687-b1f5-78cbb4a0543a).

[Primary or official source](https://sites.socsci.uci.edu/~lnarens/1990/Nelson%26Narens_Book_Chapter_1990.pdf).
