<a id="12-knowledge-waters"></a>
## Knowledge waters: a research model for revisable inquiry

Conceptual research relationship: corridors represent admissible lines of inquiry; braids compare related evidence and preserve disagreement; rivers represent sustained research trajectories and support contributions; terraces are provisional scoped reproducible understanding. New evidence or failed assumptions reopen inquiry with linked revisions. Apparent stability may conceal shared assumptions, suppressed novelty, excluded evidence or shifted burden. This is not a literal fluid law, required runtime pipeline or new Brain organ.

[SVG](svg/12-knowledge-waters.svg) · [PNG](png/12-knowledge-waters.png)

Conceptual path: corridors → braids → rivers → provisional terraces. New evidence or failed assumptions reopen inquiry through a linked revision. The old record is retained. Repair, explanation/teaching, calibration and stewardship remain visible support tributaries. Shared assumptions, excluded evidence or suppressed novelty can mimic stability.

Source-defined research equations: F_ij = w_ij ((C_i + C_j) / 2) (Θ_j − Θ_i); A_i = |Σ_j F_ij| / (Σ_j |F_ij| + ε). C denotes declared corridor density, Θ declared potential, w graph weight and A alignment ratio. These are not literal water quantities, truth probabilities or authority. Domains, estimators, versions and parameters need separate evidence.

Related existing records: F53/F86 mathematical definitions and parity; F07/F09 candidate construction and review; F10/F11 presentation; F63/F66 evidence and revision lineage; F55 transfer evaluation. No one-to-one renaming of Sonya/Sophia/Atlas is asserted.
