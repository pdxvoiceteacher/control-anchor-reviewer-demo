## Source and scope

Both translations follow English edition **2026-11-03.R6**, SHA-256 `f7e5ef46c6ac203e61d6b353f4295af0f3270b01db197c55e941b9a7c9fffe3d`. The pinned English source is the editorial comparison reference, not independent proof of its scientific claims. Stable section anchors, registry identifiers, project names, equations and technical boundaries are preserved.

The detailed family guide, term register, structured concept map and research crosswalk remain **English supplements**. Their links in each translation point to the exact published English R6 edition. These translations do not translate technical manuscript edition 1.2, the visual atlas or the whole reference bundle. Earlier translations and source editions remain unchanged.

## Translation and review

The translations were prepared with AI assistance and received separate whole-text language and technical fidelity reviews against the frozen English source. Bounded wording and terminology findings were repaired and the final bytes were checked. Source bindings, section anchors, all 86 registry rows, mathematical blocks and link destinations were also checked.

This was not fluent-human or professional translation certification. No browser, screen-reader or actual-user accessibility certification is claimed. Repository links and fragments were checked against authenticated source snapshots; primary external URLs were preserved rather than newly fact-checked. Translation review does not qualify runtime behavior or validate the underlying research.

To suggest a correction, identify the language, stable section or registry ID, and the corresponding English and translated passages. The [existing contribution guide](../../contribute/CONTRIBUTION_CHANNELS.en.md) describes the available discussion route and its CI implications. Specify R6 when discussing these translations; the preserved invitation itself remains bound to its earlier source edition.
