## Cross-document terminology risks

Across these two partial drafts, the proposed words for control, memory, community and negative results need fluent review. The ayllu and yuyay examples below occur in the whitepaper draft, not the technical-manuscript passages. In particular, using *ayllu* for community may suggest a narrower social meaning than the source intends; *yuyay* is not a validated technical-memory term. Wording for consequential use must preserve the need for permission without suggesting that only harmful uses need it. These are unresolved draft choices, not established technical equivalents.
