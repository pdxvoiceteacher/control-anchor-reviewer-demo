## F01 Triadic Brain

Integrate analysis, review, presentation, controls and voluntary cooperation around a human task.

**Input.** User task and context.
**Output.** reviewed result, evidence and permitted reuse.
**Who uses it.** The human participant and the component owners that together deliver the task.
**Who decides an effect.** The relevant local task/action owner under accepted controls and affected rights; exact current service binding is not established by this public description.
**Limit.** No single coordinator acquires every node owner's authority.

**Current scope.** Selected current or historical source mechanisms exist; full current integration and user benefit are not established for this family. Whole-family installed integration is not established by this description.
**Specific remaining join.** Demonstrate one complete governed cognitive workflow on actual user-owned machines.

**Illustrative example.** The garden group follows one task from authorized intake to a reviewed result and optional reuse.

**Further explanation.** [Whitepaper S03](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S03); this is intended behavior or a research explanation, not a deployed demonstration.
