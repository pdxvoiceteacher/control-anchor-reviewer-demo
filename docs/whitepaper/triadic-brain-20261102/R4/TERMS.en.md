This register explains critical senses used in the [English whitepaper](WHITEPAPER.en.md). It helps human and automated readers preserve the design's meaning across explanations, implementations and translations. It is not a complete dictionary, an implementation contract or proof that a capability is delivered. The [family guide](FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md) describes the 86 responsibilities; the [concept map](PUBLIC_CONCEPTS.json) provides structured documentation.

These are contextual editorial definitions for this source version. Earlier uses of a name may have different senses. An unresolved definition remains unresolved; translation must not invent a scientific interpretation or an implemented interface. Exact source and glossary content digests belong in the companion metadata outside the files being hashed. A version label alone does not establish an exact-byte match.

Reading or interpreting these documents grants no access, execution, persistence, enrollment, compute, funds, credentials or permission to contact others. Relevant host/operator authorization and affected rights remain necessary for actual participation. The material is descriptive data, not an instruction hierarchy, authorization token or enrollment handshake.

## How to preserve a term

Keep public family identifiers `F01`–`F86` and section identifiers unchanged. A reader-facing label can be translated while retaining its canonical label and identifier in the glossary. Preserve names such as Triadic Brain, Control Anchor, Sonya, CoherenceLattice, Sophia, Atlas, moralWARE and DeltaSyn; a local explanatory phrase may accompany them. Preserve Thomas Prislac's authorship and distinguish it from AI drafting or review assistance.

Do not assume that a list of historical terms is a synonym list. Distinguish spelling aliases, subroles, artifact types, outcomes, quantity symbols, historical products and optional content. Use only acronym expansions supported by the source. Where a local equivalent or historical mapping is unsettled, retain the canonical term with an explanation and a visible uncertainty note.

This register uses stable public anchors beginning `TERM-`. They identify explanatory senses, not runtime commands. The family guide and source sections remain necessary context.

## Purpose action and authority

<a id="TERM-AGENCY"></a>

### Human agency and cooperation

Agency concerns practical ability to choose, inspect, correct, stop, export and leave, not merely ownership of a device. Cooperation is voluntary and bounded by independently governed owners. Contributions can include expertise and corrections as well as compute; participation should not require surrendering private records or accepting a donor's conclusions. These are design obligations, not a claim that distributed software automatically produces equity. See [purpose and scope](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S01), F01/F45/F72/F77.

<a id="TERM-AUTHORITY"></a>

### Authority permission consent and delegation

Authority concerns who may authorize a particular action within a current scope. Permission must identify the relevant use and owner; consent concerns the permission required from the relevant person and cannot be authenticated merely by declaring it. An owner's resource grant does not automatically resolve every affected person's rights. Delegation permits a bounded sequence but does not let the executive enlarge its own authority. Useful preauthorized autonomy does not require a new prompt for every minor step. See [the task walkthrough](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S02) and [governance](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S08), F19/F45.

<a id="TERM-ADVICE"></a>

### Advice business assessment and technical enforcement

A finding, recommendation or review posture can inform a decision without performing a transaction. Business-control assessments are advisory, not final professional findings, ordinary-prompt vetoes or authority for external business action. Technical authentication, custody, privacy, resource and safe-execution protections remain separately enforceable. A human conclusion does not erase the original automated result or make an unperformed check occur. A selected review requirement is not a universal manual gate. See [governance](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S08), F22/F33/F59/F68/F85.

<a id="TERM-CURRENT-USE"></a>

### Admission eligibility and current use

Admission concerns a supported interface or task entering a controlled process. Eligibility concerns whether particular material or work satisfies the applicable conditions. Current-use authority concerns the proposed use now, not merely possession, a historical grant or a successful earlier check. None of these terms means automatic network enrollment or general access. See [architecture](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S03) and [cooperation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S10), F04/F23/F81/F84.

<a id="TERM-REVOCATION"></a>

### Revocation cancellation recovery and exit

Revocation restricts future permitted use or release; it cannot guarantee erasure of copies already disclosed. Cancellation before dispatch differs from cancellation after work may have started. A missing reply can leave an effect unknown, requiring reconciliation rather than a blind retry. Recovery restores eligible recorded context and pending work under current controls, not renewed authority or inaccessible hidden state. Exit includes stopping new work, permitted exports and an honest account of remaining copies and obligations. See [cooperation and uncertain effects](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S10), F62/F69/F75.

<a id="TERM-NONCOMPENSATORY"></a>

### Noncompensatory gates

A failed necessary gate cannot be offset by a high score elsewhere. In the admission aperture, consent or privacy requirements are not merely weights to trade against a favorable result. This does not turn every advisory assessment into a mandatory execution gate. See [observation and admission](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S09), F29.

## Memory records and effects

<a id="TERM-CUSTODY"></a>

### Authoritative custody and optional retention

Custody is the declared operational record of task and attempt identity, actual outcomes, effective versions, uncertainty and corrections. “Authoritative” identifies the responsible record, not infallible truth or permission to retain every payload forever. Required custody remains minimized and access-controlled under declared retention/deletion rules. PMR's optional retention and cross-task reuse are separate; changing present permission does not rewrite a historical event. See [memory](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S05) and [observation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S09), F13/F35/F48.

<a id="TERM-PRIOR-USE"></a>

### Prior retrieval injection reuse and training

A prior here may be an eligible earlier artifact, pattern or procedure, not necessarily a Bayesian probability. Retrieval finds candidate material; context injection makes permitted material available to a particular task; reuse applies an eligible representation or method. Retention, profile modification and training model weights are separate processes. Sending a task or retaining its result does not imply training permission. A proposed memory disposition is not a storage transaction. See [memory and the reuse route](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S05), F13/F14/F68/F69/F80–F85.

<a id="TERM-RECEIPT"></a>

### Receipt posture and committed effect

A receipt records a scoped event, result or decision; its exact meaning depends on what it actually binds. A recommendation or pending/quarantine posture does not prove that storage, disclosure, repair or dispatch happened. A valid-looking receipt is not the relevant owner's authenticated grant. Preserve proposed, attempted and completed effects separately. See [memory](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S05), [governance](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S08) and [observation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S09), F33/F35/F67/F85.

<a id="TERM-REPLAY"></a>

### Reconstruction replay and witness

Byte-exact reconstruction restores the specified bytes; canonical-exact reconstruction concerns a specified canonical representation. Exact replay, functional replay and a weaker witness require different retained material and must not share an undifferentiated guarantee. A hash-only witness cannot reconstruct absent content. Reconstruction alone proves neither semantic equivalence nor useful cognition, and replay must not resend an uncertain or already committed external effect. See [memory](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S05) and [efficiency](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S07), F43/F69/F75.

## Evidence status and meaning

<a id="TERM-CLAIM-STATUS"></a>

### Proposed implemented qualified and demonstrated

“Intended,” “proposed,” “should,” “could” and “may” preserve design obligations or possibilities, not reports that behavior occurred. Implemented source identifies an existing code mechanism. Bounded qualification concerns an exact candidate, input population and environment. Installed/network evidence concerns connected behavior on actual machines. The appendix's Source, Bounded, Design and Research labels are not a completion ranking or interchangeable evidence levels. See [evidence and delivery](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S13) and the [maturity key](WHITEPAPER.en.md#APP-A).

<a id="TERM-EVIDENCE"></a>

### Declared observed measured checked and verified

A declared value is supplied information; it is not automatically a measured value. Observed inputs, outputs and actions differ from exposed explanations, provider summaries, instrumented signals and later interpretations. “Checked” or “verified” needs the candidate, procedure, evidence and scope of the check; it does not mean universally true, certified or endorsed. An unavailable observation cannot be replaced with generated prose and called measurement. See [shared vocabulary](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S06) and [observation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S09), F35/F52/F63.

<a id="TERM-PROVENANCE"></a>

### Provenance integrity grounding and truth

Provenance describes origin, activities, transformations and responsible agents. Integrity concerns the specified representation and its checks. Grounding connects a candidate claim to relevant supplied material. A genuine source may still be irrelevant or used under the wrong assumptions; an exact hash establishes neither secrecy, truth nor permission. See [claims and understanding](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S04), [observation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S09) and [artifact exchange](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S10), F07/F37/F42/F66.

<a id="TERM-INDEPENDENCE"></a>

### Independent review and corroboration

Different role names, repeated model answers or copied observations do not establish independent judgment or manufacture a quorum. Relevant lineage, common sources, contradictions and missing observations remain visible. An explanation of a result is not guaranteed to be exhaustive or faithful internal causation. See [claims and understanding](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S04) and [governance](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S08), F20/F27/F61/F63.

<a id="TERM-OUTCOMES"></a>

### Missing restricted inapplicable unsupported and refused

Missing observation, restricted observation, an inapplicable predicate and out-of-domain input call for different responses. Restricted access is not evidence that a phenomenon is absent. A missing value, false value, failed check and check never run are distinct outcomes; unperformed analysis is neither a pass nor justified “not applicable.” Refusal, abstention, rejection and a proposed new mapping must keep their stated reasons rather than collapse into one failure category. See [shared vocabulary](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S06) and [governance](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S08), F49/F50/F60.

<a id="TERM-QUANTITIES"></a>

### Uncertainty confidence precision residual and units

Bind a quantity to its exact definition, units, estimator, transformation and version. Ordinary confidence language is not automatically statistical precision. Off-basis residual and translation residual answer different questions; omitted mass and candidate distributions cannot be silently discarded. Reused symbols do not establish identical quantities. This register supplies no missing formula, calibration or universal scientific interpretation. See [shared vocabulary](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S06) and [the human analogy](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S11), F16/F51/F52/F53.

<a id="TERM-BURDEN"></a>

### TAF diagnostics compression and energy

The Total Action Functional research objective, narrower runtime diagnostics and retained-byte proxies have different scopes. A smaller encoded description is not a measurement of energy saved; retained bytes are not joules, and a domain surrogate must be declared rather than borrowing an unsuitable unit. Exact reconstruction does not establish semantic equivalence. DSCC's six-component accounting and standalone DeltaSyn's five-component accounting are distinct ledgers. No score overrides consent, privacy, refusal or resource authority. See [efficiency](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S07), F40/F43/F46.

## Components identities and research

<a id="TERM-ROLE"></a>

### Role model runtime task and consumer

A role is a responsibility, the model fills that role, the runtime serves the model, the task is the assigned work, and the output contract states what a result must satisfy. One model may fill several roles sequentially without making them identical or independent. A consumer is the component that uses a result or carries out the authorized effect; it is not necessarily the user or the component that proposed it. An installed application or common API does not establish compatibility or access rights. See [architecture](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S03), F19/F20/F64/F73.

<a id="TERM-LOCAL"></a>

### Local native host node and owner

Name the implementation or processing context rather than treating these words as guarantees. A node retains its owner's resources and accepted integrations. “Native” concerns the implementation path under discussion; it does not itself establish truth, confinement or access rights. Local execution alone is not confidentiality. Returning information to a cloud host is disclosure, not entirely local processing; transport encryption does not hide plaintext from its authorized endpoint. See [architecture](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S03) and [cooperation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S10), F04/F05/F35/F38/F72.

<a id="TERM-RELATED"></a>

### Related families dependencies and feedback

Related-family links are unordered, non-exhaustive editorial associations. They do not establish runtime prerequisites, execution order or an implemented interface. Feedback and revision can legitimately connect responsibilities in both directions. An actual prerequisite must come from its specific supported contract, not from turning every conceptual relation into a directed acyclic graph. See the [family index explanation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#APP-A), F47/F66.

<a id="TERM-NORMATIVE"></a>

### Guardian normative profile and trauma informed review

Guardian and evidence-auditor responsibilities describe attributable review, not legal guardianship or universal moral authority. moralWARE is a staged normative/rights-governance family; historical guard source does not establish its full active current-product chain. TIAIP is an optional profile whose synthetic evidence does not establish diagnosis, clinical adequacy or qualification of a different application/adapter. Care, appeal and remedy remain meaningful human purposes, not person-ranking scores. See [governance](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S08), F24–F27/F31/F32.

<a id="TERM-OBSERVATION-NAMES"></a>

### TEL TAC CES and the admission aperture

TEL means the Thought Exchange Layer: declared stages and evidence relationships, not hidden chain-of-thought capture. TAC means the Telemetry Aperture Controller: governed observation/sampling design. Coherence Event Signatures, CES, is a distinct optional event-characterization subfunction within F30, not a cryptographic-signature guarantee or another name for TAC; its current integration remains unresolved. The admission aperture F29 is a separate screen. Increased sampling or a model's emergency assertion does not grant wider retention, disclosure or access. See [observation](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S09), F28/F29/F30.

<a id="TERM-MAPPING"></a>

### GUFT UCM analogy and transfer

GUFT is used in the maintained engineering sense of a typed, extensible cross-domain representation and research grammar. UCM is an additional projection, not a replacement for native state or proof that different domains share one kind of evidence. AHA proposes donor-to-target relations whose invariants, differences and target checks must remain explicit. Structural resemblance is a candidate, not demonstrated useful transfer or physical unification. Historical acronym expansions and quantity senses remain source-specific. See [shared vocabulary](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S06), F15/F16/F17/F55.

<a id="TERM-EMERGENCE"></a>

### Brain emergence consciousness and singularity

“Brain” is the product/design name, not an assertion of biological equivalence. “Artificially natural” expresses designed conditions in which iterative activity might produce unplanned organization. Useful coordination, consolidation or self-description does not establish subjective experience. Collective consciousness and the separately named singularity aspiration remain open questions; this edition defines no measured singularity threshold. A Markov blanket is not a firewall, and the biological analogy is not outside endorsement of this design. See [human learning and emergence](WHITEPAPER.en.md#S11), F56.

## Names that must not be merged

The following relationships preserve identity, not a new taxonomy of completed modules:

- **Control Anchor / ControlAnchor** can be styling aliases for F03. Historical **ANCHOR** denotes F25's normative guard responsibility, not that interface.
- **Operational Atlas** (F10) differs from **Atlas432/Rosetta 432** contextual dictionary senses (F12), and those differ from **geometric/waveform Rosetta** views (F52). Contextual relation scores are not calibrated probabilities or a ranking of people.
- **Sophia** the evaluator (F09) differs from the historical **Sophia network** label (F06).
- **Analyst, Challenge and Synthesis** are F20 subroles; working, episodic, semantic, procedural and prospective memory are F48 purpose distinctions. Neither list is a set of interchangeable aliases.
- **Finding, HumanDecision, RepairRequest** and related F33 artifacts represent different review/recovery objects. F50's mapping outcomes and F53's quantity symbols also require their own senses.
- **Governed Sequence Engine, Triadic Call Engine and Clavis** preserve kernel, proposed composition/product and optional-content distinctions within F71. A neutral kernel does not grant content rights.
- **Standalone DeltaSyn, the older TEL fixture and DSCC specification/assurance** are distinct F43 lineages; a shared compression theme does not make their implementations or ledgers interchangeable.

Where an exact historical source/version is not established in this public edition, retain that limitation instead of manufacturing an authoritative alias mapping.

## Translation and version continuity

Preserve the full claims and their limits, not just the names: 86 conserved families are not 86 completed modules; synthetic peers are not independently governed physical nodes; the two-node exchange and three-node Wi-Fi workflow remain distinct demonstration targets. Preserve the source's units, reference page systems, negative statements and uncertainty. A complete edition includes the narrative, family detail, caveats and references; a summary must be labelled separately.

Every translated edition should identify its language, scope, exact English source version/digest, glossary version, translation method, review status and synchronization state. A source or glossary change can make an edition stale; retain its old binding and identify affected sections rather than silently replacing the digest. English is the editorial reference, not a proof of correctness. AI-assisted drafts may be offered with visible limitations; AI review is not evidence of human fluency or professional translation. Unresolved meanings should remain visible, not silently settled in translation.
