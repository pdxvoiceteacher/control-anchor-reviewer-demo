# Triadic Brain design edition

## Cooperative human and AI cognition

This English design edition explains the intended Triadic Brain system, the Control Anchor interface and 86 preserved function families. It separates design commitments, selected component evidence and work still to be demonstrated. It is not a product release, a claim that every family is implemented, or an authorization to act.

### English reading paths

- [Main whitepaper in English](R4/WHITEPAPER.en.md): purpose, a worked scenario, architecture, memory, governance, cooperation, compact family index and references.
- [Supplementary family guide in English](R4/FAMILY_GUIDE.en.md): detailed responsibilities, examples, inputs, outputs, owners and missing connections.
- [Supplementary term register in English](R4/TERMS.en.md): definitions and distinctions that translations must preserve.
- [Structured concept map in English](R4/PUBLIC_CONCEPTS.json): descriptive data, not executable policy or a complete machine encoding of the prose.
- [Sourced standards and research crosswalk in English](R4/STANDARDS_RESEARCH_CROSSWALK.en.md): conventional terms, precise source scope and where equivalence stops.
- [Structured crosswalk](R4/STANDARDS_RESEARCH_CROSSWALK.json): 86-family comparisons without replacing their additional responsibilities.
- [English edition manifest](R4/PUBLIC_BUNDLE_MANIFEST.json): exact content bindings, review scope and known verification limits.

**Edition:** 2 November 2026, R4. This design edition adds sourced bridges to conventional scientific and engineering vocabulary while preserving project responsibilities and unresolved distinctions. It does not assert that a whole family is equivalent to a standard mechanism, that every term is novel, or that an external mechanism is implemented. Documentation review is separate from independent human assurance, product qualification, linguistic certification and rendered-accessibility inspection.
