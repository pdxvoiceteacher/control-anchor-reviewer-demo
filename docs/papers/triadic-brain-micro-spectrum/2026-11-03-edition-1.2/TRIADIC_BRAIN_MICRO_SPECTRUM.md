# Triadic Brain from Byte Identity to Revisable Knowledge

## A source bound technical account of data transformations controls and distributed inquiry

**Thomas Prislac**  
**Research manuscript local successor edition 1.2, 3 November 2026**  
**Preserved source account with a proposed reuse evaluation and autonomy objective; not published**

## Abstract

Triadic Brain is examined as an architecture for attributable, bounded and revisable inquiry. The unit of analysis is not a metaphorical cognitive particle but the smallest representation at which a specified operation is well defined: an octet string, Unicode span, application record, provider request, candidate claim, relational mapping, evidence artifact or custody event. A source-bound reconstruction follows these units through authenticated admission, separately authorized model dispatch, response capture, deterministic grounding, native assessment, review and presentation. An exact multibyte witness distinguishes original bytes from normalized source, extracted answer and derived semantic capture. The reconstruction exposes a consequential boundary: the ordinary captured-model JOINED bridge assigns unmeasured uncertainty the conservative value one, which conditionally forces its native screen to refuse even an exact quotation with complete lexical coverage and singleton softmax mass one. The answer remains inspectable. GUFT-related quantities are separated by producer, domain, units and version; historical benchmark transforms, graph-field hypotheses and current structural proxies are not collapsed into one score. An explicit analysis of the reduced river kernel shows why its actual caller step of one requires graph-dependent stability conditions that the kernel's smaller default does not establish. AHA is analyzed as source-bound checking of supplied relational bridges, while swarm and shared-grammar mechanisms are distinguished from empirical corroboration. The contribution is an auditable account of implemented transformations and unresolved research joins, together with a machine-readable claim graph. This study performs no product execution or inference experiment and makes no claim of live three-node qualification, calibrated semantic utility or autonomous action authority.

**Keywords:** provenance; cognitive architecture; representation; analogical reasoning; uncertainty; numerical stability; multi-agent inquiry; information governance.

## 1 Research question and contribution

What happens to a small piece of data when it enters Triadic Brain, and what additional warrant exists when the system later presents a useful result? Answering requires separating three questions: what representation was transformed, what procedure actually consumed it, and what inference the resulting evidence supports. A source digest can establish an identity relationship without establishing factual truth. A schema-valid analogy can preserve a relation without validating transfer to a new domain. A reviewed candidate can still lack permission for a consequential use.

The architecture's organizing research proposition is that receptive intake, structural comparison, continuity, challenge and review can operate on a common typed record while preserving native domain meanings and disagreement. The commonality is a grammar of relationships among observations, transformations, hypotheses, constraints, residuals and provenance. It is not a claim that one real-valued coordinate represents all cognition. This reading preserves the intended corridor, river, braid and terrace vocabulary as a program for organizing inquiry without treating the vocabulary as evidence of a universal physical law. [I11, I12, I15]

Four contributions organize the paper. First, a representation-level account identifies the actual byte, text, claim and artifact boundaries in a maintained application path. Second, a formal analysis distinguishes composable identity evidence from noncomposable assertions of truth, independence and permission. Third, source-specific deductions reveal a conservative screening bottleneck, distinct coherence namespaces and a graph-dependent numerical stability condition. Fourth, an explicit research program describes how distributed inquiry, shared grammar and retrospective reconstruction could be qualified without upgrading source existence into demonstrated capability.

The surrounding literature supplies comparison points, not borrowed validation. W3C PROV-DM gives entities, activities, agents and derivations a precise provenance vocabulary. Gentner's structure-mapping theory motivates examination of relational organization rather than surface similarity. Calibration research distinguishes normalized scores from probabilities that correspond to empirical correctness. None of those works establishes the adequacy of this particular architecture. [PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/), [Gentner 1983](https://groups.psych.northwestern.edu/gentner/papers/Gentner83.2b.pdf), [Guo et al 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html).

## 2 Method and evidence classes

### 2.1 Source reconstruction rather than a new execution study

The analysis uses a selected, privately retained source closure, separately retained byte identities, historical qualification reports and reconciled research documents. A refreshed accepted-source comparison found only documentation changes relative to the preceding mapped runtime snapshot. The selected inventory contains 163 reading copies; 157 match their separately retained original Git blobs exactly. Six connector-derived reading copies contain one additional final LF; removing exactly that byte produces separate reconstructed source copies whose full Git blob identities match the pinned originals. The private source map preserves the reading copies, reconstruction receipts and distinct hashes rather than substituting one identity for another. These facts support a bounded source reconstruction, not a full fresh repository audit or deployment attestation. [I01]

No product module was imported, no project test or CI job was run, no provider was called, and no installed client or live peer was exercised for this study. Calculations on the synthetic byte exhibit and elementary analytic deductions are distinct from product execution. The exhibit is deliberately incomplete as a runtime record: it has no authenticated actor, effective deployment profile, actual provider response, event clock or completed human review. Values depending on those inputs remain symbolic. [I01, I04]

The evidence vocabulary is deliberately multidimensional:

| Code | Evidence class | What it establishes | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| S | Inspected source | A definition, branch or contract exists in the identified source | Execution, installation or utility |
| C | Inspected call path | A specified producer is connected to a specified consumer within the inspected closure | That this path was invoked in a deployment |
| T | Authored test | A check and intended fixture population exist | A passing result |
| R | Retained execution report | A source-bound report records a bounded historical execution | Independent rerun or current-source parity |
| D | Analytic deduction | A conclusion follows from stated source equations and assumptions | An observed production outcome |
| P | Proposed mechanism | An intended interface or experiment is specified | Implementation or adoption |
| H | Research hypothesis | A conjectured relationship or formal model is specified; empirical testing may still require operational definitions | Empirical validity |
| U | Unavailable or unobserved | The relevant evidence has not been established here | Zero, falsehood or permission to invent a value |

A claim can have several codes, for example S+C+D. A test-ready empirical hypothesis additionally names its observables, scope and potential falsifiers; a formal definition alone need not yet supply them. The chain S→C→R→deployment→utility is not an implication chain. A mechanically sound implementation of a poor estimator remains a poor estimator. [I01, I11]

## 3 Representation types and the smallest meaningful unit

Let \(\mathbb B^*\) be finite octet strings, \(\mathbb U^*\) finite Unicode scalar sequences, \(\mathcal J_p\) the JSON values accepted under profile \(p\), and \(\mathcal A\) typed application artifacts. A transformation is modeled as a partial function

\[
f:(x,\kappa,\pi)\rightharpoonup(y,e,z),
\tag{1}
\]

where \(\kappa\) is interpretation context, \(\pi\) the effective control context, \(e\) evidence of the operation, and \(z\) a status. Refusal, unavailable input and uncertain completion are represented states; they are not silently replaced by successful values. This is an exposition model, not a claim that every implementation function has this literal signature.

An octet is the smallest addressed unit in the presented byte contracts. A Unicode code point is the unit used by text coordinates. A provider token is a tokenizer-defined unit whose mapping is model- and template-dependent. A lexical token type is a different unit used by the native citation diagnostic. A claim span is a bounded textual proposal; a relation mapping is the unit of the supplied AHA case. A custody event is a recorded observation with local ordering. A TCP segment or TLS record belongs to a transport layer, not to these semantic units. UTF-8 allows a character to occupy multiple octets; TCP provides a byte stream rather than preserving application record boundaries. [RFC 3629](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3629.html), [RFC 9293](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9293.html).

For an artifact \(a\), the useful minimal semantic carrier is therefore relative to a question. To verify an excerpt, one needs at least its text, source identity, interval convention and source bytes or an adequately trusted verification record. To evaluate an analogy, one needs relations, mapped endpoints, domain restrictions and a target test. Removing those dependencies may shorten a message while making the intended operation undefined. There is no source-supported universal smallest semantic packet. [I04, I05, I09]

Define byte identity and profile identity separately:

\[
H_B(b)=\operatorname{SHA256}(b),\qquad
H_p(x)=H_B(C_p(x)),
\tag{2}
\]

where \(C_p\) is an explicitly versioned canonical encoding. For noncanonical incoming JSON \(b\), generally \(H_B(b)\ne H_p(\operatorname{parse}(b))\). SHA-256 detects byte changes relative to a trusted reference under its cryptographic assumptions. It does not authenticate the original author or validate a proposition in the bytes. [FIPS 180-4](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/fips/180-4/upd1/final).

The inspected canonical writers use compact sorted-key UTF-8 JSON, finite numbers and one final LF. Their accepted domains differ. One requires already-NFC strings and rejects surrogates; the native profile additionally rejects specified controls and default-ignorable characters and applies key restrictions. Some parser entry points accept ordinary JSON whitespace, with canonical byte equality checked at later boundaries. Integer 1 and floating 1.0 can compare equal in a host language while serializing to different bytes. These are project profiles, not an assertion of JSON Canonicalization Scheme conformance. RFC 8785 is a useful comparison precisely because numeric and Unicode rules are part of a canonicalization contract. [I02, I05]. [RFC 8785](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785.html).

Figure 1 presents the inspected ordinary captured-model JOINED route. Boxes are logical owners or artifact stages, not fixed physical machines or a claim that each is a separate model.

```text
          |              |

                 |

                 |

                 |

          |                    |
   CAPTURE_ONLY return     CAPTURED + JOINED
                               |
                    deterministic semantic bridge
                               |
                grounding / claims / UCM / AHA
                waveform / counterexamples / aperture

                               |
                   Atlas orientation and TEL closure

```

**Figure 1.** Static call-path reconstruction. Non-model events use a separate admission/completion path with no model candidate. Terminal provider observations other than CAPTURED do not become native candidates by implication. Neither the diagram nor an artifact label supplies authority for later action. [I03–I10]

Intake combines a bounded source, task, supplied evidence and explicit consent, writes exact task bytes before submission, and can bind selected complete prior conversation pairs. The inspected contextual-reuse path limits prior messages to 16 and canonical context to 16,000 bytes; it rejects overflow rather than silently truncating. This is explicit selection, not proof of general memory retrieval. The gateway enforces a 32,768-byte task body ceiling and derives the actor from authenticated application context. Source text cannot authorize itself by claiming a trusted role. [I03]

The gateway forwards base64-encoded task bytes to custody. For \(n\) input bytes, padded base64 length is \(4\lceil n/3\rceil\) characters. This is reversible encoding, with overhead, not encryption or semantic compression. Custody records an attempt before admission, including bounded submitted bytes and their identity. Record idempotency binds actor and key to exact raw payload semantics: reuse with changed bytes conflicts. Rejected and uncertain attempts remain part of the observed population. [I03]

Admission freezes an effective control profile and runtime context, validates task/privacy/provider authorization, constructs normalized grounding, and invokes AEGIS. Legacy non-observation modes also execute the UCC admission plan; the inspected observation profile retains that compatibility plan as a definition only and performs separate advisory business assessment later, while technical input, role, privacy, capacity and dispatch boundaries remain enforced. Thus “controlled” does not mean that every named policy is an executable veto in every mode. Here the application privacy check verifies explicit task consent and agreement with the configured policy identifier. The native privacy gate carries that declared basis and checks its Boolean/basis contract; it does not independently classify sensitive content or establish policy adequacy for every affected person or recipient. A successful parse does not convert untrusted source instructions into authority. [I03]

Dispatch requires the exact queued record, version and request hash. Transactional claim rechecks relevant limits and bindings, constructs the provider request body and reserves accounting before invocation. A reservation proves neither remote completion nor a charge. Restarted in-flight requests become UNKNOWN rather than being blindly repeated. The engineering rationale resembles least privilege and complete mediation, but the presence of those design principles is not a security certification. [I06]. [Saltzer and Schroeder 1975](https://web.mit.edu/Saltzer/www/publications/protection/Basic.html).

## 5 An exact multibyte witness

### 5.1 Bytes and coordinates

Take the illustrative source ` Café.\r\n` and task `Quote the source.`. The source contains a leading U+0020, the already-NFC character U+00E9, and CRLF. Stipulate, without making a model call, that extracted answer text is `Café.`. The normalizer in the application bridge is

\[
N(s)=\operatorname{NFC}\!\left(\operatorname{CRtoLF}
 (\operatorname{CRLFtoLF}(s))\right).\operatorname{strip}()+\mathrm{LF}.
\tag{3}
\]

NFC concerns canonical Unicode equivalence; newline substitution and boundary stripping are additional application operations. They should not be attributed to the Unicode normalization standard. Direct native grounding has another contract: it converts newlines and strips, but requires already-NFC input rather than silently applying NFC. The initial canonical intake can also reject non-NFC input before the later bridge. This witness is already NFC and does not demonstrate acceptance of an arbitrary decomposed spelling. [I02, I04, I05]. [Unicode UAX 15 revision 57](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/tr15-57.html).

| Representation | UTF-8 hex | Code points | Bytes | SHA-256 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Original source | `20436166c3a92e0d0a` | 8 | 9 | `4068c2d9de08c36023e33a7858bf0d9e36bfa39017edd2af2d1927ed413e2498` |
| Normalized source | `436166c3a92e0a` | 6 | 7 | `ead8f00c3b7dd22fe4ea6e1679b51bbbe2696e72b0692a278bf100aae0928b9d` |
| Extracted answer | `436166c3a92e` | 5 | 6 | `7d84df6634452499da807a641b387dbcb28193ef940a6fb297ac86c652d5ca75` |

These are calculated identities of synthetic bytes, not captured inference evidence. U+00E9 occupies two UTF-8 bytes. The answer's interval is [0,5) in code points and [0,6) in UTF-8 bytes. These are zero-based half-open coordinates in the normalized source, not grapheme-cluster, UTF-16, tokenizer or original-file coordinates. [I04]

In this route the native grounding builder receives the already-normalized seven bytes. Consequently its source and normalized-source hashes coincide, while the original nine-byte identity remains in the separate admission binding. A lower-level caller passing the original bytes directly would produce a different native-source identity. A paper that calls every one of these “the raw source” loses the distinction it purports to explain. [I04, I05]

Equation (3) is many-to-one. For example, boundary whitespace distinctions disappear. Therefore a general inverse \(N^{-1}\) does not exist. Retaining the original hash records which original was admitted only when the trusted custody binding is available; the digest alone cannot reconstruct discarded bytes. This establishes a concrete information-loss boundary without claiming the operation preserves meaning in whitespace-sensitive languages or formats.

### 5.2 Expansion and artifact identity

The short source is not the full model input. An illustrative source/evidence/task wrapper has 161 UTF-8 bytes, and its one-message canonical JSON array has 200 bytes. A fabricated provider-shaped response body used only to illustrate serialization has 91 bytes and an explicit NOT_EXECUTED identity. No tokenizer counts, latency, provider identity or packet capture are inferred from these sizes. The complete request envelope and provider configuration remain unspecified. [I04]

Native grounding segments normalized text by paragraph boundaries, with a line-based fallback for one multiline paragraph. It does not infer minimal propositions. The witness produces one segment with text `Café.`, independent code-point and byte offsets, and the excerpt digest above. The corresponding constructed segment JSONL is 243 bytes, its manifest 525 bytes, and the derived semantic-capture JSON 684 bytes. These larger artifacts carry coordinate, identity and control information. They illustrate overhead from accountability, not a measured end-to-end efficiency ratio. [I04, I05]

Serialization creates further identity domains. Bare answer bytes have length six; native JSON scalar `"Café."` plus LF has length nine; object `{"text":"Café."}` plus LF has length 18; an ASCII-escaped comparison spelling has length 22. Omitting the object's terminal LF gives 17 bytes and a different digest. Equal decoded strings do not imply equal serialized artifacts. The machine-readable witness records exact bytes and hashes for each comparison. [I04]

## 6 Model processing and what the trace cannot see

### 6.1 Adapter and transport semantics

Supported provider profiles define bounded, nonstreaming text contracts. A generate-style adapter can serialize role-labeled history into one prompt string, whereas chat-style adapters send message arrays and another profile uses typed text input/output blocks. The existence of a common application envelope does not make provider tokenization or chat templates identical. Response normalization checks the supported completion shape: complete text can become CAPTURED; explicit refusal remains REFUSED; HTTP errors become FAILED; incomplete, length-limited or unsupported output can become UNKNOWN. Tool calls and unsupported modalities cannot be silently interpreted as the supported text response. [I06]

The maintained transport retains application response-body observations, not Ethernet frames, IP packets, TCP segments, TLS records or complete HTTP header bytes. HTTP framing and TLS record protection are separate layers. A library-delivered chunk is neither a stable network packet nor a model token. The inspected configuration disables HTTP/2, declines redirects, ignores environment proxies and requests identity content encoding; these are source settings, not measurements of a session. The 2018 TLS 1.3 specification is cited here as a layer reference, not as an assertion about a particular negotiated connection or the latest specification. [I06]. [RFC 9112](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9112.html), [RFC 8446](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8446.html).

For external routes, source specifies approved public-address resolution, numeric connection target with official Host/SNI, TLS verification, connected-peer and certificate-digest observations, and bounded request, response, deadline and cleanup. Local routes have separate loopback constraints. Known-secret echo checks can withhold a body but do not establish comprehensive data-loss prevention. Transport protection does not make source/context values secret from their authorized compute endpoint; §15 distinguishes that visibility and retained-copy scope. Observed body bytes and retained-prefix bytes have separate lengths and hashes, so a bounded prefix is not mislabeled a full body. [I06]

### 6.2 Conditional model internals

For a transformer-based runtime, a useful background decomposition is

\[
u\xrightarrow{\text{format}_t}\tilde u
\xrightarrow{\tau_v}(t_1,\dots,t_n)
\xrightarrow{\text{embedding}}H_0
\xrightarrow{F_{\theta,b}}H_L
\xrightarrow{\text{head/decoding}}(\hat t_1,\dots,\hat t_m)
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{decode}_v}\hat u.
\tag{4}
\]

Here \(v,t,\theta,b\) denote tokenizer/version, input-formatting/template, parameters and backend context, not observed values. The named decoder does not assume a bijective inverse to tokenization. A standard attention block uses

\[
\operatorname{Attn}(Q,K,V)
=\operatorname{softmax}(QK^\top/\sqrt{d_k}+M)V.
\tag{5}
\]

In equation (5), \(d_k\) is the query/key dimension and M is the additive attention mask; softmax operates over keys. Equations (4)–(5) are architectural background. They do not identify the actual runtime's layers, quantization, hidden activations, KV state or decoding randomness. The original Transformer work establishes this attention construction, not the implementation of any private deployment. [Vaswani et al 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762v7).

The inspected application trace exposes none of the actual token sequence, applied template expansion, internal tensors, backend numerical error or training updates. Reported usage counts are provider claims subject to contract validation. Hosted weight identity is unobserved; local configured or readiness identity is not execution attestation. Thus model generation is a causally important but internally opaque transformation in this account. The native bridge's deterministic analysis is inspectable downstream and must not be mistaken for a view into the model's latent computation. [I06]

### 6.3 Completion as a product of states

Use the explanatory state product

\[
Z=(z_{admit},z_{dispatch},z_{body},z_{norm},z_{eval},
z_{posture},z_{review},z_{use},z_{payload},z_{cleanup}).
\tag{6}
\]

A body can be complete while cleanup is uncertain. Remote inference may have occurred even if local capture acknowledgement is lost. Historical execution can remain complete after retained payload expiry. CAPTURED can coexist with native REFUSE and a reviewer HOLD. Cancellation records an attempted intervention, not proof the remote computation ceased. Exact identities enable reconciliation rather than an unsafe assumption that an unacknowledged request never executed. This factorization is an analytical model of source distinctions, not a new unified runtime state schema. [I06, I10, I14]

## 7 Native transformations from answer text to a reviewed candidate

### 7.1 Deterministic claims and evidence binding

The semantic bridge normalizes extracted terminal text and splits stripped paragraph or line spans into claims. It does not split on periods, avoiding some decimal and abbreviation errors but also making no claim to proposition extraction. For each span it searches grounding segments for an exact substring. A first exact match yields one candidate-declared SUPPORTS reference; no match yields no reference. The operation proposes quotation support, not semantic entailment or exhaustive source selection. [I05]

The bridge records three separate identities: upstream provider body, normalized provider envelope and derived semantic capture. The captured-candidate adapter's downstream “raw output” is that derived semantic JSON. Its quarantine contains 684 bytes in this witness, not the original provider body. The adapter is a local facade with no model invocation. Conflating the two adapters would falsely imply that a downstream audit independently re-read live response bytes. [I05, I07]

The following data contract is an **illustrative projection**, not an executable product request or the literal full candidate schema:

```json
{
  "representation": "claim_excerpt_projection",
  "evidence_class": "CONSTRUCTED_NOT_EXECUTED",
  "text": "Café.",
  "coordinate_domain": "normalized_source",
  "code_point_interval": [0, 5],
  "utf8_byte_interval": [0, 6],
  "source_sha256": "ead8f00c3b7dd22fe4ea6e1679b51bbbe2696e72b0692a278bf100aae0928b9d",
  "excerpt_sha256": "7d84df6634452499da807a641b387dbcb28193ef940a6fb297ac86c652d5ca75",
  "declared_relation": "SUPPORTS",
  "semantic_entailment_established": false,
  "authority_effect": "NONE"
}
```

Claim validation reconstructs source/segment identity, excerpt identity, claim-text identity, interval containment and byte/code-point agreement; overlapping references within a claim are marked invalid. The support category follows citation integrity and the declared relation. A fixed lexical tokenizer uses Unicode word-like spans, casefolding and English stop words. Its unique token-type coverage is a diagnostic, not the provider token count or a factual likelihood. For the witness the claim and excerpt each reduce to `{café}`, giving coverage one and no residual token types. [I05]

Candidate identifiers use a 24-hex prefix of the SHA-256 of the ASCII concatenation of two full hexadecimal digest strings. The seed therefore contains 128 ASCII bytes, not 64 binary digest bytes. That prefix is a 96-bit label; the full artifact digest remains the identity used in integrity claims. Grounding bundle labels similarly use a 20-hex prefix. Truncated labels must not quietly replace full artifact hashes in proofs or collision arguments. [I05]

### 7.2 UCM ranking and the conservative uncertainty invariant

Let \(r\) be the fraction of claims not marked unsupported, and \(u\in[0,1]\) the candidate uncertainty field. Default structural axes are

\[
a=(E_{cpl},T_{tr},E_s,\lambda_{phase},\mu_{contain})
=(r,1,1,1-u,r),\qquad \psi_{CL}=E_{cpl}T_{tr}.
\tag{7}
\]

These are source-defined dimensionless proxies. The names do not establish empirical ethical symmetry, psychological empathy or physical phase stability. Supplied full-axis metadata and caller-declared hypotheses are separate inputs under the native contract. [I07]

For a nonempty population of finite scores \(s_i\) partitioned by declared equivalence groups \(g\), UCM computes

\[
p_i=\frac{\exp(s_i-s_{max})}{\sum_j\exp(s_j-s_{max})},\quad
P_g=\sum_{i\in g}p_i,\quad
m=P_{(1)}-P_{(2)}.
\tag{8}
\]

Group masses are ordered descending, and \(P_{(2)}=0\) when only one group exists. The complete population determines the result; top-k affects presentation only, with retained and omitted display mass recorded. The code calls \(p_i\) posterior probabilities, but the input scores are caller-declared logits, not measured log likelihoods. A singleton necessarily receives mass one, even if the sole alternative is wrong. Normalization is not calibration, a distinction also central to empirical calibration research. [I07]. [Guo et al 2017](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html).

The implemented precedence is noncompensatory. REFUSE applies if authenticated source references are absent, transparency is below .20, uncertainty is at least .85, unsupported claims remain, or any supplied hypothesis is out of distribution. Otherwise HOLD applies for \(\psi_{CL}<.45\), ethical-symmetry proxy below .50, uncertainty above .40, equivalence margin below .10, or new/ambiguous pattern posture. Remaining cases yield PASS_SCREEN, with human review required and authority NONE. These thresholds are source facts, not validated scientific decision boundaries. [I07]

**Conditional invariant.** Every successful invocation of the inspected ordinary captured-model JOINED builder sets \(u=1.0\), explicitly as unmeasured conservative maximum. If the unchanged native UCM builder and screen are then reached with valid artifacts, \(u\ge.85\), so REFUSE follows regardless of the other axes or logits. For the witness, \(r=1\), \(a=(1,1,1,0,1)\), \(\psi_{CL}=1\), \(p_1=1\), and \(m=1\), yet the uncertainty gate refuses. This is S+C+D evidence about this route, not an observed error rate or a global statement about every response. [I07]

CAPTURE_ONLY does not execute this native branch. Non-model events have no model candidate. A lower-level captured API can receive another schema-valid uncertainty under a different contract. None of those facts justifies fabricating a lower uncertainty to make the ordinary path pass. An alternative estimator would need its own definition, calibration population, failure handling and qualified downstream integration. The current conservative behavior preserves inspection while declining epistemic promotion.

### 7.3 Synthetic waveform and bounded counterexample search

The current reference waveform encodes the five ordered axes as

\[
x_j=\operatorname{round}_{12}\!\left[
\frac15\sum_{k=1}^5a_k\sin(2\pi kj/N)\right],\quad
E_{codec}=\operatorname{round}_{12}\left[N^{-1}\sum_jx_j^2\right].
\tag{9}
\]

The sampling domain is integer \(N\in[16,4096]\), with \(j=0,\ldots,N-1\) and default \(N=64\). Phase is cycles per synthetic record, not time in seconds. Before rounding, discrete orthogonality gives \(E_{codec}=\sum_k a_k^2/50\) at these sample counts, hence .08 for the witness. This deduction is not a measured waveform execution. Different axis vectors can have equal energy, so energy alone loses allocation information. No physical frequency of a person, model or archetype is measured, and cross-domain utility is explicitly unestablished. [I08]

The counterexample stage lists unsupported claims and searches source-segment lexical tokens for a finite limitation-marker set. The witness yields no matches. That zero means no detected match under this bounded method; it is not exhaustive falsification, adversarial model debate or proof that the proposition has no counterexample. [I08]

### 7.4 Aperture review and useful output

Aperture evaluates hard gates for task consent, the carried privacy-policy predicate, optional PMR retention consent, grounding, context, quarantine, claim evidence, UCM nonrefusal and AHA nonrejection. A failed hard gate yields REFUSE before softer holds are considered. On otherwise-valid witness inputs, the UCM gate alone fails. Missing AHA is retained separately but does not replace the hard-gate reason. PMR is off in this ordinary route, while required evidence custody remains active. “No PMR write” therefore cannot mean “no content was stored.” [I08]

Sophia separately recomputes selected artifact relationships, including spans, claim map, full UCM projection, residuals, waveform, AHA, counterexamples, aperture and TEL. It uses a local numeric tolerance of \(10^{-12}\) where specified; byte-hash equality is another condition. It reads raw-free verification receipts rather than quarantine bytes and explicitly reports that it did not independently verify those absent bytes. It does not rerun UCC policy predicates. [I10]

For the conditionally valid witness, correctly recorded UCM/aperture refusal and unavailable AHA produce Sophia HOLD, not necessarily REJECT. ### 8.4 A coherent cancellation donor and a testable target hypothesis

## 9 Corridors rivers terraces and numerical consequences

### 9.1 Research specification and reduced implementation

The graph-field research specification distinguishes corridor density C, river density R, terrace density \(T^{terr}\), potential Θ, memory M and instability terms. It defines

\[
F_{ij}=w_{ij}\frac{C_i+C_j}{2}(\Theta_j-\Theta_i),\qquad
A_i=\frac{|\sum_jF_{ij}|}{\sum_j|F_{ij}|+\varepsilon},
\tag{16}
\]

and a river evolution law of the form

\[
\dot R_i=D_R\sum_jw_{ij}(R_j-R_i)
-\operatorname{div}_G(R\nabla_G\Theta)_i
+C_iA_i(\alpha_R+\beta_RM_i)-\mu_RR_i
-\nu_R([\Delta S_i]_++[\Lambda_i]_+)R_i-\chi_RR_i^3.
\tag{17}
\]

A terrace law adds transported diffusion, a stability-weighted river source, memory/coherence support, loss and cubic saturation. These equations preserve the design intentions of exploration, accumulation and provisional stabilization. They require explicit graph orientation, discretization, boundary conditions, coefficient units, estimator definitions and admissibility semantics before numerical or scientific claims follow. The boundedness \(0\le A_i<1\), for finite inputs and positive ε, is an arithmetic consequence of the triangle inequality, not validation of “agreement.” [I12]

The inspected reduced kernel is materially simpler. For an undirected edge list with weights w it computes

\[
(LR)_i=\sum_jw_{ij}(R_j-R_i),\quad
g_i=\frac{\sum_jw_{ij}(\Psi_j-\Psi_i)^2}{\sum_jw_{ij}},
\tag{18}
\]

with g set to zero when the denominator is nonpositive, then

\[
R_i^{n+1}=\operatorname{clip}_{01}
\{R_i^n+h[D_r(LR^n)_i+\alpha g_i-\beta R_i^n]\}.
\tag{19}
\]

Kernel defaults are \(h=.05,D_r=.08,\alpha=.6,\beta=.1\), with optional clipping. The inspected evolution caller explicitly supplies **h=1.0**. It subsequently calls the reduced terrace update with the same step,

\[
T_i^{n+1}=\operatorname{clip}_{01}
\{T_i^n+h(\kappa R_i^{n+1}-\mu g_i-\nu T_i^n)\},
\tag{20}
\]

whose kernel defaults include \(h=.1,\kappa=.15,\mu=.10,\nu=.03\). In this caller, the terrace phase consumes the newly updated, clipped river field \(R^{n+1}\); it recomputes g from the Ψ field unchanged between these phases. This is a sequential update, not a simultaneous old-state update. Neither equation implements all transport, memory and cubic terms in the fuller specification. A heuristic bridge producing named rivers is yet another producer, not a discretization of equation (17). [I12]

### 9.2 Stability at the actual caller step

The following deductions assume finite nonnegative undirected weights, fixed Ψ, \(D_r\ge0\), \(\beta>0\), and no clipping. Let \(L_+\) be the positive graph Laplacian, so \(L=-L_+\). Then

\[
\dot R=-(D_rL_++\beta I)R+\alpha g,
\qquad R_* =\alpha(D_rL_++\beta I)^{-1}g.
\tag{21}
\]

In an eigenmode with Laplacian eigenvalue \(\lambda_k\), explicit Euler multiplies error by \(1-h(D_r\lambda_k+\beta)\). The standard explicit-Euler stability disk supplies the framework; applying it to this source equation gives the strict-decay condition

\[
0<h<\frac{2}{D_r\lambda_{max}+\beta}.
\tag{22}
\]

[Driscoll and Braun on absolute stability](https://fncbook.com/absstab-diffusion/).

With the actual h=1 and default coefficients, equation (22) requires \(\lambda_{max}<23.75\). The kernel default h=.05 instead requires \(\lambda_{max}<498.75\). For nonnegative initial R and nonnegative forcing \(\alpha g\) as under the stated defaults, a sufficient coefficientwise positivity condition is \(h(D_rd_i+\beta)\le1\) for every weighted degree \(d_i\), giving \(d_i\le11.25\) at h=1. Positivity and strict spectral decay are different properties. Neither establishes an upper bound of one without further conditions on the forcing. [D from I12]

An exact counterexample to universal stability uses a hypothetical two-node graph with one edge of weight 20 and equal fixed Ψ, so \(g=0\) and \(\lambda_{max}=40\). The difference-mode multiplier is \(-2.3\) at h=1, versus .835 at h=.05. Under clipping, start with \(R^0=(1,0)\). The unprojected next state is \((-.7,1.6)\), so projection gives \((0,1)\); the following step returns \((1,0)\). Boundedness coexists with an exact period-two orbit in this isolated, fixed-input kernel. This is an algebraic constructed example, not an executed test or a claim about an actual deployed graph.

Without clipping, summing equation (21) cancels symmetric diffusion and yields

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\sum_iR_i=\alpha\sum_i g_i-\beta\sum_iR_i.
\tag{23}
\]

River density is not generally conserved. Clipping contributes a projection correction. Uniform weight scaling changes diffusion stiffness while leaving the normalized g unchanged for positive scaling. Duplicate mirrored edges can likewise change accumulation. A valid numerical evaluation must retain graph identity, weights, step, preprojection increments and projection residuals. No actual graph spectrum or run in this study establishes that the production caller was unstable. [I12]

### 9.3 Recursive inquiry and representational regimes

The master research recursion is

\[
X_{n+1}=\mathcal M\circ\mathcal S\circ\mathcal R(X_n),
\tag{24}
\]

where receptive input, structuring and continuity/metabolism act on evolving task state. These functional passes are not a one-to-one identification with three named components or three fixed models. State may retain hypotheses, contradictions, budget and unfinished tests; repeating a prompt alone does not establish refinement. [I12]

A reduced complex-state proposal uses \(Z_n=\Psi_n+iK_n\) and \(Z_{n+1}=\rho_ne^{i\theta_n}Z_n+\eta_n\). For constant \(a=\rho e^{i\theta}\ne1\) and constant η, \(Z_*=\eta/(1-a)\) and \(Z_n-Z_*=a^n(Z_0-Z_*)\). Thus \(|\rho|<1\) contracts perturbations; \(|\rho|>1\) generically expands them. For \(|a_n|\le r<1\) and bounded forcing \(|\eta_n|\le B\),

\[
|Z_n|\le r^n|Z_0|+B(1-r^n)/(1-r).
\tag{25}
\]

This bound does not ensure convergence to one value, and rotation can move the real coordinate outside [0,1]. Calling a regime river or terrace does not settle those mathematical issues. A flat vector field is not an equilibrium unless the defining fixed-point or zero-derivative condition is met. [I12]

Broader ecology formulas relating transfer, branching, capture, plurality and renewal remain H-class definitions. Their unresolved scalar/vector types, denominator domains and empirical estimators are substantive research work. Fuller ecological derivations and operationalization remain outside this paper’s detailed analysis; these formal proposals are not all test-ready empirical hypotheses. They are not used here to score people or institutions. ### 10.1 A discovery spiral from unresolved evidence

In the Waters of Gnosis research program, an unresolved condition can identify an unexplored direction as well as a limitation. Unmeasured uncertainty can reveal an absent observation; conflicting evidence can distinguish competing explanations; an awkward donor-to-target mismatch can expose a relation worth formulating. The constructive aim is innovative discovery and novel AHA candidates, with the original uncertainty preserved rather than converted into confidence. An unknown value is not evidence that the candidate is false, and disagreement is not sufficient evidence that either side is wrong. Neither is evidence of novelty by itself. [I09, I12, I15]

The proposed spiral makes this aim operational in the original knowledge-waters vocabulary. A **corridor** records a bounded alternative or question. A **tributary** supplies a targeted observation, calibration, repair or contrasting source. A **river** records a developing line of inquiry across iterations. **Braiding** keeps complementary relations and material dissent connected while preserving their separate lineage; it does not average them into forced agreement. A **terrace** represents provisional support within a stated scope, and **rupture or reopening** revisits that support when new observations undermine its conditions. These are inquiry functions that can be expressed through existing workbench roles and supplied AHA cases; this paper does not require new compulsory components or identify them with physical flow. [I12, I13, I15]

A proposed bounded sequence is to retain the unresolved condition and its evidence basis; let the user choose a question, permitted sources and exploration budget; formulate alternative relations or candidate donor mappings; challenge the specific assumptions that distinguish them; and return a new candidate, preserved dissent and the next discriminating observation. Successful exploration may reveal a previously unarticulated relation even while the original candidate remains held. It may instead establish that a corridor has little support. Those are different useful outcomes. The exploration state must therefore track discovery candidates separately from validated findings and action permission. [I09, I12, I13, I20]

This is P/H-class integration and test design, not a current automatic feedback loop. The ordinary JOINED refusal rule stays unchanged. A held result does not dispatch further calls, authorize new recipients, or turn a supplied structural map into proof. The user retains control over whether to explore, which branch to follow and when to stop; each consequential operation still needs its own applicable authority. A matched evaluation could compare a residual-guided spiral with ordinary clarification under the same data and budget, using separately adjudicated useful new hypotheses, newly supported relations, retained dissent and review burden. Mere novelty of wording, increased output count or increased agreement would not establish the proposed benefit. [I07, I13, I15, I20]

## 12 Local workbench and distributed swarm

### 12.1 Roles and dependence

The local Analyst→Challenge→Synthesis workbench binds selected same-root prior artifacts, source/control versions and explicit selection. Synthesis must preserve material agreement, disagreement and unresolved challenges rather than treating role consensus as truth. Historical loops making three or seven differently prompted calls to one model show prompt diversity and repeated sampling; they do not establish distributed independent cognition. Distinct role names and even distinct models cannot by themselves prove independence of evidence. [I13]

To illustrate why this matters, suppose equal-variance errors have variance \(\sigma^2\) and common pairwise correlation r, with an admissible positive-semidefinite covariance matrix. Then

\[
\operatorname{Var}(\bar\epsilon)
=\frac{\sigma^2}{m}[1+(m-1)r],\qquad
m_{eff}=\frac{m}{1+(m-1)r}.
\tag{26}
\]

The displayed effective sample size assumes a positive denominator; its singular zero-variance boundary is not assigned a finite value. For general weights w and covariance Σ, variance is \(w^\top\Sigma w\). These are explanatory deductions, not implemented confidence estimators. Shared training, source exposure and seeing a prior answer can create dependence. Neither verbal agreement nor model labels measure r. Benchmark gains reported for multi-agent debate motivate controlled experiments but do not imply that more agents or rounds universally improve outcomes. [I13]. [Du et al 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/du24e.html).

N work items with concurrency c are not N simultaneous workers. All ordered-pair comparisons cost \(N(N-1)\) before rounds. A user event with N analytical children is one source event and N jobs, not N+1 independent observations. A qualified workbench therefore needs the denominator: attempted, failed, stale, cancelled and unresolved jobs as well as accepted outputs. Targeted challenges of distinct assumptions may be preferable to unbounded all-to-all exchange. These are design consequences, not measured speed or quality claims. [I13]

### 12.2 Artifact transfer and peer model seams

Bounded peer artifact transfer exists as an authenticated edge with exact archive/reference/offer binding, accepted pairing, current grant, original deadline, TLS and receiver-owned verification. Custody-issued digest references outside an archive are not signatures. A package whose bytes verify can still contain a false statement, and a separately authenticated verifier role does not imply independent model corroboration. [I14]

Peer-model sender, receiver and request/status/result contracts also exist. The inspected source contains default-off one-attempt sending, default-off receiving, current-grant projection and native custody hooks. Bounds include canonical request/status/result frames at most 65,536 bytes, original job windows at most 120,000 milliseconds, declared output limits at most 4,096 tokens, and returned text at most 32,768 UTF-8 bytes; the result envelope has its own frame bound. The receiver requires an injected authenticator. Execution requires a separately injected native owner that checks current policy, trusted clock/resource ownership and transactional reservation. Legacy intake may retain a held receipt without that owner, while structured intake rejects ownerless admission; neither intake outcome supplies execution authority. A grant-shaped object in a payload cannot grant itself authority. [I14]

However, the maintained paired transport allowlist inspected for this study routes artifact receiving/verification/cancellation, not peer-model jobs. No production construction of the model receiver or sender invocation was established in the selected closure. The evidence supports real bounded implementation seams plus missing end-to-end joins. It does not support a qualified live three-node cognitive swarm. A three-node pilot remains proposed or partially implemented until each edge's transport, owner, grant, custody and result-consumption evidence is established. Connectivity and permission are not transitive. [I14, I20]

External Challenge retention and local synthesis consumers preserve exact payload references while keeping delivery observations separate. Current use/sharing rights are rechecked independently of historical completion. This distinction is necessary for both accurate provenance and bounded reuse. [I14]

### 13.1 Three different observation domains

### 13.2 Provenance composition does not compose truth

A conceptual provenance graph can use entities for bytes and assessments, activities for transformations, and agents for responsible producers or reviewers. This corresponds to PROV-DM's subject matter without claiming implementation conformance. [PROV](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-dm/).

For a result y, distinguish six predicates:

\[
\operatorname{BytesMatch}(y),\quad
\operatorname{SenderAuthenticated}(y),\quad
\operatorname{LineageBound}(y),\quad
\operatorname{ProcedureObserved}(y),\quad
\operatorname{EvidenceAdequate}(y,q),\quad
\operatorname{UsePermitted}(y,p,r,t).
\tag{27}
\]

The last predicate depends on purpose p, recipient r and time t. None of these predicates entails all the later ones. Their conjunction may be necessary for a particular use, but it is not a universal theorem of truth. Source integrity, interpretation and action authority have different owners and failure modes. [I03, I10, I14, I16]

Replicated evidence is not automatically replicated-log consensus. Raft addresses agreement on log state under a specified protocol, not agreement on scientific truth. Similarly, Certificate Transparency specifies inclusion/consistency proofs and concerns about inconsistent views; a local digest chain is not thereby a globally witnessed transparency service. These are contrast classes, not claimed components. [Ongaro and Ousterhout 2014](https://www.usenix.org/conference/atc14/technical-sessions/presentation/ongaro), [RFC 9162](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9162.html).

### 14.1 Reference communication with residuals

Recovered historical mechanisms include a versioned dictionary with support bindings, domain/unit-preserving transform records, and byte copy/residual reconstruction with explicit cost accounting. ### 14.2 Retrosynthesis as dependency-directed reconstruction

Here retrosynthesis means tracing a desired or produced result backward through the transformations and support needed to justify or reproduce it. It is an analytical description of useful provenance traversal, not a claim that a general inverse-reasoning engine is already implemented. Let \(f_y=\operatorname{pr}_y\circ f\) be the output projection of the tuple-valued transformation in equation (1), with the evidence/status contract separately fixed. For \(y=f_y(x,\kappa,\pi)\), define the admissible predecessor set

\[
\operatorname{Pre}(y)=\{(x,\kappa,\pi):f_y(x,\kappa,\pi)=y
\text{ under the declared identity and status contract}\}.
\tag{28}
\]

For a many-to-one normalization, Pre can contain multiple byte histories. A hash reference plus an unavailable original cannot restore those histories. For stochastic or opaque inference, reproducing exact output may additionally require unobserved runtime state. The honest retrospective result can therefore be “identity recorded, payload unavailable,” “structural recomputation available,” or “runtime inference not reproducible,” rather than an invented full replay. [I04, I06, I15]

For a claim q, let \(\mathcal S(q)\) contain the declared adequate, nonempty alternative support sets under a chosen method and scope. Adequacy is a separate set-level scientific judgment, not something created by the availability/permission checks below. With purpose p, recipient r and time t explicit, an explanatory present-use condition is

\[
\operatorname{SupportUsable}(q,p,r,t)
=\bigvee_{S\in\mathcal S(q)}\ \bigwedge_{e\in S}
\left[\operatorname{Available}(e,t)\land
\operatorname{UsePermitted}(e,p,r,t)\land
\operatorname{MethodApplicable}(e,q)\right].
\tag{29}
\]

An empty family \(\mathcal S(q)=\varnothing\) yields false, and empty members are excluded; an evidence-free logical theorem would require its own declared justification rather than accidental vacuous support. Equation (29) is a proposed dependency semantics, not an implemented global evaluator. It shows why losing one source need not falsify q when independent adequate support remains, and why a historical record does not automatically authorize present reuse. A general dependency-aware reopening engine, minimal repair-set solver and cross-node revocation propagation require further implementation and qualification. [I15, I20]

Retrospective reasoning can nevertheless guide useful output today: identify the exact candidate and input version, expose which checks actually ran, preserve dissent, enumerate missing evidence, and formulate a bounded next test. A route that returns a clarification request but executes no repair should say so. The artifact becomes an accountable workbench state rather than a claim that uncertainty has vanished.

### 14.3 Provenance reuse and autonomy in shared work

The research hypothesis is that permission-respecting reuse of a supported artifact can reduce repeated derivation at comparable accepted quality and coverage. Identifiable origins, versions, dependency mappings and corrections may help recipients assess what can be reused. Additional copies alone do not supply stronger evidence or independent corroboration. Reuse remains subject to the authority, dependence and privacy boundaries of sections 12–15. [I13–I17]

A proposed comparison would fix the same repeated-task workload, source snapshot, required outputs, hardware/runtime conditions, stopping rules and observation horizon for two arms. The recomputation arm derives each required result anew under a declared policy. The reuse arm resolves a prior artifact, verifies its identity, support, applicability, freshness and permissions, then adapts it with recorded residuals or falls back to recomputation. Preregister acceptance criteria and a quality-comparability margin; use blinded adjudication where feasible. Report accepted outputs alongside attempted, rejected, failed, stale, cancelled and unresolved tasks. Equivalent-service savings require comparable accepted quality and coverage.

For each resource unit u, define the descriptive difference ΔC_u = C_recompute,u − C_reuse,u over that whole workload. Both totals include attributable initial production, retrieval, verification, adaptation, storage and replication, transport, coordination, failed attempts, fallback work and later corrections within the declared horizon. Charge each resource event once, including work shifted to peers. State shared-setup and persistent-storage allocation; distinguish cold-start totals from warm-cache results. Positive ΔC_u denotes a saving only in that measured unit and scope, subject to quality and coverage comparability. [I15, I19, I20]

Report compute in directly measured operation counts or hardware-qualified device-seconds, energy in joules when instrumented, traffic in bytes, storage occupancy in byte-seconds, elapsed latency in seconds and human review in person-minutes. Keep dimensions separate; elapsed latency is not summed parallel compute. Tokens, calls and retained-response bytes do not establish FLOPs, energy or correctness probabilities. Missing measurements remain unavailable. [I16]

Practical autonomy is a primary design objective evaluated separately from resource savings: the ability to exit, choose a model or provider, retain local control and participate in accountable cooperative governance. A study should specify the required capabilities and assess whether participants can exercise them, including failures and barriers. Resource savings do not compensate for violating a declared minimum rights requirement, and users may prefer greater autonomy despite higher cost. Decentralization alone establishes none of these outcomes. This is a normative design commitment with operational evaluation criteria, not a favorable term added to the cost difference. The proposed study has not been executed; no new efficiency or autonomy result is reported here. [I19, I20]

## 15 Security privacy and failure analysis

The threat model includes malicious source instructions, malformed artifacts, forged identity assertions, stale grants, partial delivery, replay, duplicate execution, correlated reasoning errors, misleading metric names, privileged custodian compromise and unresolved copies outside the local owner. This paper evaluates design boundaries, not exploit resistance of an installed product.

| Failure or threat | Inspected boundary | Residual limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Source text requests privileged actions | Source treated as data; authenticated actor and separate dispatch authority | Prompt framing alone does not prove model obedience or eliminate injection |
| Changed payload reuses an idempotency key | Exact raw-payload binding | An uncertain prior outcome still requires reconciliation |
| Truncated response is called complete | Observed/retained lengths and hashes; provider-specific completion checks | Remote execution and local acknowledgement can diverge |
| A citation is mistaken for truth | Exact span/digest reconstruction; human-review posture | Source adequacy, truth and semantic entailment remain separate |
| Metrics compensate for policy failure | Noncompensatory aperture gates | Profile-specific business controls can be advisory |
| A second role is counted as independent evidence | Same-root history and explicit contribution identity | Statistical dependence is not measured by labels |
| A forged grant authorizes peer work | Injected authenticated owner and current policy checks | End-to-end production model route not established |
| Local log is called immutable consensus | Hash chain, append-only schema, sealed inventory | Privileged host/custodian and external observation remain trust dependencies |
| A raw-free bundle is called anonymous | Some raw bytes excluded from review input | Derived source and answer text can remain identifying |
| Payload expiry is called complete erasure | Scope-controlled payload expiry and tombstones | Append-only events and external copies can retain content |

[I03, I05–I10, I13–I17]

These controls do not establish confidentiality from an authorized compute endpoint or a privileged local host. The selected paths construct readable source/context/request values and retain content-bearing artifacts; TLS protects transport between endpoints. This study establishes no confidential-computation or independently verified remote-retention guarantee and does not audit unrelated host protections. Route selection therefore requires deciding that the recipient and retained-copy scope are appropriate for the data. [I06, I14, I17]

The final row is concrete. Selected custody BLOBs and derivative files can expire subject to custodian confirmation, legal hold and active-evaluator checks. However, append-only attempt events can contain original request bytes, context-binding events can contain effective messages, and capture-commit events can contain normalized response text. The inspected expiry routine does not remove all those event details. Exports, backups, providers and peers have separate copy lifecycles. A claim of complete content erasure would therefore be false for the inspected scope. [I17]

Hashes themselves can be linkable, especially for low-entropy candidate texts. A raw-free receipt is an omission boundary, not de-identification. A review bundle can preserve source and answer content while excluding provider body bytes. Privacy analysis must inventory content-bearing copies and authority owners rather than count only files labeled raw. These limitations do not imply that existing role, retention and dispatch controls are absent; they define what those controls actually cover. [I17]

Fail-safe defaults, least privilege and complete mediation remain useful criteria: missing authorization should not become permission; each use should be checked at the responsible owner. They guide review without proving all implementation paths satisfy them. [Saltzer and Schroeder 1975](https://web.mit.edu/Saltzer/www/publications/protection/Basic.html).

## 16 Research qualification agenda

The next evidence should close specific joins rather than maximize the number of impressive labels. Each proposal below is P-class and requires its own authorized scope; none was executed for this paper.

1. **Pinned representation experiment.** Use a permitted tokenizer/template/runtime build and synthetic fixtures with multibyte text, normalization variants and whitespace-sensitive tasks. Retain exact bytes, token IDs where observable, rejection points and complete contracts. Separate tokenizer visibility from hidden model computations.
2. **Screening calibration.** Define an uncertainty producer with an explicit target event and population. Evaluate discrimination, calibration, refusal tradeoffs and adverse cases on held-out tasks. Compare to the current conservative route without changing a threshold merely to increase pass rate.
3. **Numerical qualification.** Pin graph topology, weight semantics, coefficient versions and actual caller steps. Analyze the spectrum or a justified sufficient bound, retain unclipped updates, compare step refinements and report projection residuals. Separately test whether densities predict any useful cognitive outcome.
4. **AHA target test.** Freeze donor/target graphs and mappings before observing target outcomes. Record disanalogies, comparator, falsifier, cost and stopping condition. Compare structural reviewability with observed target validity rather than treating them as the same endpoint.
5. **Workbench dependence experiment.** Compare single-pass, repeated single-model, role-diverse and genuinely source-diverse conditions under matched budgets. Blind adjudication where feasible; retain failures and dissent. Do not infer independence from different role labels.
6. **Distributed qualification.** Establish each peer edge's installed entry point, authenticator, resource owner, current grant, bounded transport, transactional reservation and receiver observation. Include lost acknowledgements, stale grants and payload expiry. Report a three-node pilot only after its full stated population is qualified.
7. **Lifecycle and copy accounting.** Inventory all content-bearing owners, verify exact expiry scope and preserve truthful tombstones. Test correction and current-use decisions against alternative support graphs. Do not promise recall of copies outside the enforcing boundary.
8. **Outcome and cost evaluation.** Define task success before inspection, compare matched baselines, separate factual support from usability, and report human review burden, latency, bytes and actual measured resource costs. Unavailable energy or total-action measurements remain unavailable. For the sharing hypothesis in section 14.3, compare permission-respecting reuse and recomputation at matched quality and coverage, including retrieval, verification, storage, replication and coordination. Evaluate practical freedom and cooperative control separately from resource savings.

The independent review sequence should freeze the manuscript and machine companion, evaluate source/call-path fidelity, evaluate mathematics and units, evaluate security/privacy/distribution, evaluate scientific claims, and compare every machine claim with prose. Material corrections require a new version and targeted rereview. This is publication governance for this paper, not evidence that the system has automatically performed those reviews.

## 17 Discussion and conclusion

The smallest meaningful data unit in Triadic Brain depends on the operation. Bytes make content identity checkable; Unicode spans make excerpts locatable; application records make actor and task bindings explicit; claims and relational maps make proposed interpretations reviewable; event and artifact graphs make derivations attributable. These units can be composed without pretending that identity, truth and authority are one property.

The source reconstruction shows concrete native machinery: durable attempts, bounded dispatch, provider-specific capture, deterministic source binding, structural screens, independent bounded recomputation and inspectable output. It also reveals sharp limits. The ordinary JOINED bridge's conservative uncertainty fixes a refusal condition; a single normalized probability does not certify correctness; a waveform codec does not measure cognition; a structural analogy does not execute its falsifier; a local log does not supply distributed consensus; and bounded payload expiry does not establish total erasure.

The GUFT and cognitive-waters program remains scientifically substantive when its quantities retain native definitions and its hypotheses retain falsifiers. The reduced graph kernel, fuller field proposal, historical telemetry and current UCM proxies are distinct objects. Their relation is an engineering and research question, not an equality licensed by notation. The analytical two-node example demonstrates the practical importance of examining actual callers and projection behavior, even when outputs remain bounded.

The resulting architecture can be understood as a workbench for revisable knowledge: preserve observations and alternatives, make transformations inspectable, expose uncertainty, and keep consequential use under its own authority. That is the paper's supported account and its research direction. Demonstrated semantic gain, live three-node cooperation, calibrated cross-domain transfer and comprehensive dependency lifecycle control remain evidence to be obtained.
