## Problem and bounded milestone

Prospective users include small organizations and local software builders who
need to inspect AI-assisted work and retain a review history. Demand and
usefulness have not been established. A possible smaller milestone would improve
inspection and reproducibility for the **existing offline evidence tool**:

- Clarify evidence identities, scripted decisions, open findings and verification
  limits so reviewers can follow a captured record and revision.
- Provide reproducible intact/modified/missing-evidence examples using the real
  directory and ZIP consumers, including ZIP member verification versus exact
  live-transaction ZIP-byte binding.
- Trace one existing interrupted-transition recovery path and document a bounded
  synthetic example with its observed outcome. Do not imply general crash recovery
  or success if that consumer has not been exercised.

Acceptance would be a small reviewed change, executable examples with explicit
positive/adverse outcomes and a readable local guide. Identify the exact consumer,
qualified engineering and controls/design responsibilities, paid hours,
supervision where needed, infrastructure and full engagement costs before setting
a request. Do not presume a student-led plan or unpaid excess work. The optional
[University Student Intern Opportunity](INTEGRATION_PILOT.md#university-student-intern-opportunity)
would be separately costed, paid and supervised if pursued.
